About DJPJ

DJPJ is a free auto-DJ that runs in your web browser. Pick a genre or search for a song, and DJPJ builds a never-ending, beat-matched mix with transitions and effects. There's no app to install and no account to create.

DJPJ is free to use. Tips are optional and help cover the costs of running the site.

Contact

Questions, refund requests, rights concerns or feedback: email djpjapp@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Tips & refunds

Tips are voluntary gifts to support DJPJ. They don't buy access to any feature, product or service. Tips can be between $2 and $500. Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay tips appear on your statement as DJPJ APP.

Refund policy

  • Request within 3 days. To get a tip back, email djpjapp@gmail.com within 3 days of tipping. Include the amount, the date, and how you paid (Apple Pay, Google Pay or Venmo).
  • Refunds are net of processing fees. Payment processors keep their fees when a payment is refunded, so approved refunds are the tip amount minus those fees (for card, Apple Pay and Google Pay tips, about 2.9% + 30¢).
  • After 3 days, tips are final.
  • Mistakes and unauthorized charges are always refunded in full, whenever you let us know. This includes duplicate tips, a wrong amount, or a charge you didn't make.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually take 5–10 business days to appear. This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the law or with your bank or card issuer.

Privacy

DJPJ is built to collect as little as possible.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups or passwords.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, Apple, Google or Venmo.

What's stored on your device

Your mix settings (music source, vibe, clean-lyrics option) are saved in your browser's local storage so they're remembered next time. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Services DJPJ connects to

To play music and take tips, your browser talks directly to these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and basic browser information, and each follows its own privacy policy:

  • Audius, Apple (iTunes previews) and the Internet Archive: song search and audio.
  • Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Venmo: tips. If you tip, we receive the amount, date and payment confirmation, plus whatever name or email the payment provider shares. We use this only to process the tip, handle refunds and keep required records.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare: hosting and network security, which may keep short-term technical logs.
  • Google Fonts: the site's typefaces.

If we add visitor statistics or ads in the future, this policy will be updated first. We don't sell personal information. DJPJ isn't directed at children under 13.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email djpjapp@gmail.com.

Music & rights

DJPJ doesn't host any music. Tracks stream directly from Audius (independent artists' full tracks), Apple's iTunes (30-second previews, linked to Apple Music so you can get the full song) and the Internet Archive (recordings in the US public domain). All rights belong to the artists and rights holders.

If you're a rights holder and want something removed or credited differently, email djpjapp@gmail.com and we'll act on it promptly.
